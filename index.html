Scene 01 Credit

Now showing · Statlog German Credit

Credit risk decisions, not just a leaderboard

Python · scikit-learn · Streamlit

A lender does not get paid for a high AUC. On the public Statlog German Credit file, funding a defaulter is treated as five times as costly as turning away a borrower who would have paid. The desk question is who should be approved.

$22,800
Illustrative holdout cost at a cutoff of 0.15. The rule books 56 of 200 applicants, and 5 of those default.
$37,200
Saved versus approving every holdout applicant. A 0.50 cutoff costs $27,200 on the same 200 people.
8.9%
Default rate inside the booked loans, 5 of 56, against a 30% base rate in the file.

Gradient boosting was selected on training-fold ROC-AUC (0.793 ± 0.054) before the holdout. Holdout ROC-AUC is 0.772. A holdout cost flip toward the random forest was reported and was not used to change the model.

Limit. Dollars translate the published Statlog matrix at 1 cost unit = $200. They are not expected loss. This is a 1994 German installment file, not a 2026 U.S. application population.

Scene 02 Lending

Now showing · Lending Club sample

Loan approval prediction

Python · scikit-learn · Streamlit

An educational loan-decision project. A scikit-learn pipeline scores a public Lending Club sample and turns a stated dollar loss into an approve or decline cutoff. Fully paid is the positive class. A false approval is weighted at $5,000 and a false decline at $600.

$137,000
Saved on the 1,916-loan holdout versus declining everyone. The scored cutoff costs $828,400, against $965,400 for declining everyone and $1,535,000 for approving everyone.
94.8%
Of 443 approvals, 420 were fully paid. The 0.64 cutoff approves 23.1% of the holdout.
0.704
Holdout ROC-AUC for logistic regression, the model the selection rule named. Cross-validated ROC-AUC was 0.670 ± 0.015.

The holdout ranked the three tuned models the same way the training folds did. The dollar gap between them is about $7 a loan. The gap that matters is the one against the rules that ignore the model: $71.50 a loan versus declining everyone.

Limit. The weights are a planning scenario. The file has no charge-off balance. A score of 0.64 is the cutoff on this model, not a 64% chance the loan repays. Holdout Brier score is 0.223, worse than guessing the 84% fully-paid base rate.

Scene 03 Fraud

Now showing · OpenML credit card

Credit-card fraud review lab

Python · scikit-learn · Streamlit

After exact duplicates are removed, the book is 275,190 legitimate transactions and 473 frauds. Approving every transaction is already 99.83% accurate, and it still pays every fraud. The lab decides which tickets to pull into a manual review queue, and it scores that decision in dollars.

$10,903
Saved versus approving every test transaction. The selected random forest costs $6,411. Approving everything costs $17,315.
76 of 95
Test frauds sent to review, with 25 legitimate tickets pulled into the queue.
18.3
Reviews per 10,000 transactions. Precision 0.752 and recall 0.800 at the cutoff locked on validation.

The selection rule was locked before the test set: lowest validation dollar cost, ties broken by validation PR-AUC. That rule names the random forest. Test PR-AUC for that forest is 0.679.

Limit. The 95% bootstrap interval on the forest’s test cost is $2,102–$12,180. An undersampled logistic regression costs $5,728 on the test set and was not selected. This is a case study, not a production fraud system.

02Skills

Tools and practices that show up in the public repos and in the bookkeeping work. No proficiency bars.

Decision models

scikit-learn pipelines: logistic regression, random forests, gradient boosting, and histogram gradient boosting, with an isolation forest kept as a late-label baseline. Cutoffs are chosen on out-of-fold or validation cost and then frozen. The write-ups report ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, Brier score, and permutation importance, including the times a holdout ranking disagrees with the precommitted choice.

Data and delivery

pandas, Jupyter, and Streamlit. pytest, Ruff, and GitHub Actions. Public files used as documented: Statlog German Credit, a Lending Club OpenML sample, the OpenML credit-card extract, and UCI Online Retail. The revenue repo also includes a Next.js planning dashboard.

The books and the web

QuickBooks Online, as a QuickBooks ProAdvisor. Tax preparation under an active PTIN. Double Eagle Financial LLC, bookkeeping for small businesses. HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the marketing sites. Go High Level for the talking-website samples.

03About
